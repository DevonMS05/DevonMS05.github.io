About Me

Hi, I'm Devon!

I'm an artist, a businesswoman and currently a creative director for an editorial magazine. I'm also a student at Far Eastern University, taking ACT Networking 1.

It's a mix of different worlds but each one gives me something new to learn. I like having different sides to myself.

I've been working since I was 16 years old. Work has been part of my life for a long time but that doesn't mean I have everything figured out. I'm still learning, asking questions and finding out what works for me.

A little thing people might not expect, I'm an introvert. I can lead a creative project and handle business but I also enjoy having space to myself. Being quiet and having big ideas can definitely go together.

Hobbies and Interests

Drawing was my very first hobby. Before the job titles and responsibilities, there was simply me enjoying what I could make on a blank page. It's a part of me and I'd love to make room for it again.

Now, being a creative director gives that interest a different kind of space. I get to think beyond a single drawing and consider how images, ideas and stories come together in a magazine.

Things That Keep Me Curious

  • Exploring creative ideas.
  • Discovering different styles of art and design.
  • Learning how to turn ideas into a business.
  • Understanding technology and networking.
  • Trying something new, even as a beginner.

My interests don't always fit into one category, and honestly, I like that. My schedule may disagree but my curiosity enjoys the variety.

Goals and Aspirations

Right now, I want to grow as a creative director, continue building my business and do well in my studies. I want to understand my craft more deeply and become more confident in my decisions.

What I'm Working Toward

  • Developing my skills in editorial design and art.
  • Growing my business thoughtfully.
  • Learning more about networking through my course.
  • Finding a healthy balance between work and school.
  • Making time to create something just for myself.

I also want a life that has room for things that aren't work. I started working young and while I'm proud of that, I want to remember that not every hobby needs to become a business and not every quiet moment needs to be productive.

I don't have one perfect picture of where I'll end up. I just I want to keep creating and building in a way that glorifies God and stay open to discovering new sides of myself.

And hopefully, I'll have time again to draw something just because I feel like it.
